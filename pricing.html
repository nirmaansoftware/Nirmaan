How pricing works

No meter running.

The same four rules on every project, whatever its size.

  1. Step 1

    Free first conversation

    Tell us the problem. We ask questions, separate facts from assumptions, and tell you honestly if you don't need custom software at all.

  2. Step 2

    One fixed price

    A written proposal with what's in scope, what isn't, the timeline and the price, fixed before any work starts.

  3. Step 3

    Pay as it progresses

    40% on approval, 40% when you review the working version, 20% on delivery.

  4. Step 4

    Changes priced up front

    New ideas mid-project are welcome. Each one comes with its cost and time before any work starts.

Pricing questions

The small print, big.

Why “from” prices?

Because the problem sets the price, not the page count. The “from” price is what the simplest version of that kind of project costs. You get a fixed quote after we understand what you need, before you commit to anything.

Do prices include GST?

Prices are shown before GST. Where GST applies, it's added on the invoice and shown separately in your proposal.

What if my budget is lower?

Tell us. We'll usually suggest a smaller first version that solves the most painful part of the problem, and grow it later.

Is a care plan required?

No. It's optional and you can start or stop it at any time. If you'd rather host and maintain the system yourself, we hand over everything you need.

Who owns the code?

You do, once the project is paid for: source code, database and every asset.

Not sure which one fits?

Describe the problem. We'll price the fix.

Most people don't know whether they need a website, an app or an automation. You don't have to. Tell us what's going wrong and you'll get a fixed quote for the simplest system that solves it.
