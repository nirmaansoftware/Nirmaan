What a Nirmaan build contains

Not just the front end.

Most studios hand over a website. We build what sits underneath it too: five layers, one system, one team accountable for all of it.

5 / 5

  1. Database PostgreSQL · MySQL

    Structured storage designed around your data, not bolted on later. Fast to query, backed up, secured.

  2. Backend logic Node.js · Python

    The rules only your business has: pricing, approvals, calculations, workflows. Written as real code, not plugins.

  3. APIs & integrations REST · Payments · Webhooks

    Clean interfaces between your system and everything it talks to: payment gateways, CRMs, messaging, partners.

  4. Automation Schedules · Triggers · Reports

    The repetitive work nobody should do by hand. Imports, notifications, reports and syncs that just run.

  5. Interface HTML · React · Next.js

    The website or app your customers and staff actually touch. Fast, accessible and mobile-first.
