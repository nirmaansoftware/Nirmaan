# Legal and Compliance

Findings are labelled [Technical observation]. No legal advice is given. A
Legal and Compliance pass is an agent review, never a legal sign-off.

FAIL when: legal text changes without a decision record; a change to
terms.njk makes it disagree with the published prices; a new data flow is not
covered by the privacy notice; a client, partner or prospect is named without
linked consent; docs/legal/** drafts lack "DRAFT, not reviewed by counsel"; a
change to privacy.njk or terms.njk does not notify the IP council chair.
legal-counsel, privacy-counsel and licensing-counsel hold no key; their
comments are advisory.
