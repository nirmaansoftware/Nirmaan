# Commercial

A Commercial pass is an agent review, never a price decision: prices, names
and sending stay with the founder.

FAIL when: published prices disagree with terms.njk; a price change does not
match a recorded decision, or GST treatment is not stated (finance-estimation,
tax-accounts); a claim about results or reply rates appears
(sales-partnerships); a client, partner or prospect is named without linked
consent (client-communication, sales-partnerships); the human send gate
(founder-only approve and send) is weakened, or a do-not-contact entry can be
bypassed (client-communication).
