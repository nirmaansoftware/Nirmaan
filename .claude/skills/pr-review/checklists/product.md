# Product

FAIL when: a capability, status or date is not supported by
docs/product/<product>.md or docs/assessment/current-state.md; a requirement
has no PRD or decision record; a PRD marked ready-for-handoff has an open HIGH
question; MVP scope grows without a decision; inferred rules are not marked
INFERRED; market facts lack a dated primary source, or assumptions are stated
as fact (market-research); real client data appears under projects/**; a dated
decision record or docs/history/** file is edited other than by adding a dated
"Superseded by" note.
