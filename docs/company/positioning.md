# Positioning · IMPLEMENTED on nirmaan.online (2026-09-24)

## The line

> You bring the problem.
> We build the system.

Supporting line: *From websites and apps to automation, AI systems and custom
business software, Nirmaan turns real-world problems into reliable software.*

Primary action: **Tell us your problem.** Secondary: **See what we build.**

## What makes it true (and has to stay true)

| Claim | Backed by |
|---|---|
| The customer doesn't need to know the technology | Problem-first intake: the first question is the problem, never "website or app?" |
| We understand before we build | Discovery separates facts, assumptions, questions and recommendations; nothing is built on a guess |
| Simplest system that works | Proposal guidance and the Solution Architect principle ("complexity must earn its place") |
| You stay in control | Client approval of scope and price, plan, go-live and handover |
| Proof, not claims | Requirements traced to tests with recorded evidence |

*Note (2026-10-18, [market learnings decisions](../strategy/market-learnings-decisions.md), D1):*
the OS records the client's own decision on scope and price (the proposal).
Sign-off on the plan, design, go-live and handover is taken from the client in
writing and attached as evidence before the founder approves that gate (see
[project-lifecycle.md](../operations/project-lifecycle.md)). An OS record per
milestone is PLANNED (backlog ML17).

## Voice

Technically capable, practical, human, calm. Short sentences, concrete
examples ("orders lost in WhatsApp"), plain words.

**Never:** "leading IT company", "cutting-edge", "digital transformation
experts", "best-in-class", AI hype, invented statistics or clients. Also never
(added 2026-10-18, [market learnings decisions](../strategy/market-learnings-decisions.md)):

- "unlimited";
- speed or cost multiples ("3x faster", "40% cheaper");
- any share of the work done by AI ("80% built by AI", "AI builds your app");
- "AI-powered" or "AI-native" as a label for Nirmaan, or a human name or
  persona for an AI;
- "pre-built modules", feature counts or a "library" in a proposal or on the
  site, until an asset is rated proven in
  [reusable-ip.md](../strategy/reusable-ip.md);
- "secure", "hack-proof", "bank-grade", "100% secure", "100% tested" or
  "audited" as outcomes, and any certification we do not hold;
- struck-through or "was" prices;
- uptime percentages, "real time" or "24/7".

A speed number is allowed only with a linked run anyone can repeat (device,
throttling, date).

## How we talk about AI

Describe AI by what a person does with it and who answers for the result:
"AI-assisted, human-accountable". Never describe it as a share of the work, a
speed multiple or a persona. The OS records model calls, tokens, cost and
latency; it cannot measure a share of work, so no such figure is ever
published. If a simple rule does the job better than AI, say so.

## What stays off the public site

Internal margins and costs, AI spend, the agent roster and orchestration
design, prompts, proprietary workflows, reusable IP, customer scoring,
security architecture, and operational metrics. The former public "Agency OS"
page was removed for this reason; its customer-relevant ideas (human approval,
evidence, change requests, accountable AI use) now live on the Process page.
