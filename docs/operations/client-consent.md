# Client consent register · IMPLEMENTED (first entry 2026-11-03)

A client's name, a link to its site or a case study may appear on a Nirmaan website only after the client has agreed. This page records each client's consent, so a review can check it.

The founder takes the consent: by message, by email or on a call. The founder's entry here is the record, and it may say how consent was given. Private evidence such as messages and call notes is never kept in this public repository.

When a name is added here, it is also added to the `client-names` rule in [`.github/review-teams.yml`](../../.github/review-teams.yml), so any later change that mentions the client goes to Legal and Commercial.
