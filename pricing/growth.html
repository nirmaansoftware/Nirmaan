Growth questions

Asked often.

Which payment gateways do you work with?

The common Indian gateways, such as Razorpay, Cashfree or PayU. We'll recommend one based on your fees and how you get paid out.

Can I move from Instagram or WhatsApp orders?

That's the most common reason people come to us. We import your products and set up an order flow your team can actually follow.

How many products can it handle?

Hundreds without any special work. If you have thousands of products or complex variants, we'll say so in the quote.

Why is it a “from” price?

Because the problem sets the price, not the page count. The “from” price is what the simplest version of that kind of project costs. You get a fixed quote after we understand what you need, before you commit to anything.
