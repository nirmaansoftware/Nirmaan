Platform questions

Asked often.

Is ₹80,000 really enough for a platform?

For the simplest real version, yes, which is what the “from” price means. Most platforms cost more; the fixed quote after discovery tells you exactly how much, and why.

Can we launch in phases?

We recommend it. A first version that solves the core problem goes live sooner, and the next phases are shaped by how people actually use it.

How do you handle AI costs?

Every AI feature has a budget and its usage is measured. You see what it costs to run before you commit to it.

Why is it a “from” price?

Because the problem sets the price, not the page count. The “from” price is what the simplest version of that kind of project costs. You get a fixed quote after we understand what you need, before you commit to anything.
