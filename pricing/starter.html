Starter questions

Asked often.

Can I update the site myself?

If you want to, yes: we can make the text and images you change often editable. Otherwise send changes to us; they're part of a care plan, or quoted individually.

I already have a domain. Can we keep it?

Yes. We connect the new site to your existing domain and keep your email working while we switch over.

Will it show up on Google?

It's built to be found: clean structure, fast pages, proper titles and descriptions. Ranking also depends on competition and content over time, so we won't promise a position.

Why is it a “from” price?

Because the problem sets the price, not the page count. The “from” price is what the simplest version of that kind of project costs. You get a fixed quote after we understand what you need, before you commit to anything.
