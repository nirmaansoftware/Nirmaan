The problem

Finding customers is the hard part.

Most small businesses look for new customers in one of three ways. Each works, up to a point.

  • 01

    Ads

    You pay for every click. The day you stop paying, the enquiries stop too.

  • 02

    Bulk messages

    Lists and blasts get ignored, reported and blocked, and they take your name down with them.

  • 03

    Doing it yourself

    Personal outreach works. Researching each business and writing to it takes hours you don't have.

Nirmaan Marketing does the research and the first draft. You keep the judgement.

What it does

The research and the writing, done for you.

Everything that makes personal outreach slow, handled, with the decisions left to you.

  • Finds your customers

    Searches maps and the web for businesses that match the customer you describe, in the cities you choose.

  • Looks for a real reason

    Reads each business's own website for a problem you can solve, like bookings only by phone or a site that breaks on phones.

  • Writes like a person

    A short first message for each business, in your voice: one true observation and one small next step. No templates.

  • You approve every word

    Nothing goes out until a person has read the exact message. Edit it, approve it, or skip it.

  • Sends like a person

    Email goes from your own mailbox, in working hours, one at a time with natural gaps, well under your mailbox's daily limit.

  • WhatsApp, done right

    Each WhatsApp message is prepared for you and sent from your phone in one tap, the way WhatsApp's rules allow. Your number stays safe.

  • Follows up, then stops

    A short follow-up in the same thread a few days later. A reply stops everything at once.

  • Listens to replies

    Replies become leads. Anyone who says “stop” is never contacted again. Bounced addresses are retired.

  • Learns what works

    Every week it moves effort toward the customers, cities and channel that reply, and tells you what it changed.

Built in, not optional

Outreach that protects your name.

These rules are part of the system. They can't be switched off by accident.

  • Every message ends with a one-line way to opt out, and it can't be edited away.
  • Anyone who says stop is never contacted again, by email or WhatsApp.
  • Only contact details a business has published itself. No bought lists, no personal numbers.
  • Plain text with your real signature. No tracking pixels.
  • A few messages per business at most, days apart, and nothing after they reply.
  • Every search, draft, approval and send is on record, with who approved it.

Questions

What people ask first.

Is this spam?

No. Spam is the same message sent to everyone. Each message here is written for one business, about something true on its own website, approved by you, with a way to opt out. Anyone who says stop is never contacted again.

Will WhatsApp ban my number?

Not from this. Sending WhatsApp messages automatically to people who haven't written to you first breaks WhatsApp's business rules, so it never does that. It prepares each message, and you send it from your phone in one tap, starting with a few dozen new chats a day.

Do I have to write the messages?

No. The research and the first draft are done for you. You read, edit if you like, and approve, usually in batches, in a few minutes a day.

Does it use AI?

Yes, to research businesses and draft messages. A person approves every message, and nothing it reads on the web can make it do anything.

What do you need from me?

A mailbox to send from, your WhatsApp Business number, and an idea of who you want to reach. We'll help you sharpen the last one.

What does it cost?

A fixed quote to set it up for your business, after a free conversation. Running costs depend on how many businesses you want to reach, and we show you those numbers before you start.

Do you use it yourselves?

Yes. Nirmaan finds its own clients with it. That's why it exists.
