The brief

“We need a website.”

Sudaangeo came to us for a professional web presence: something that explained the business and let customers get in touch.

What we found

The real work was happening by hand.

Once we understood how the business actually ran, it was clear the bigger opportunity sat behind the website: operational data being processed manually, day after day.

What we built

A system, with a website as its front door.

We built a custom data-processing and business-automation tool that now does real operational work. The database, the automation logic and the workflows that used to be manual were all designed around how Sudaangeo specifically operates, not a generic template stretched to fit.

Where it is now

Live, and still improving.

The system is in daily use. We keep supporting it and iterating as the business changes.
